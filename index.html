The Vision

What if your AI actually
knew you?

Not your search history. Not your preferences file. You. Your businesses, your people, your decisions, your patterns, your voice, your trajectory. What if it was always listening for its name, and when you said it, it answered with real intelligence about your real life?

That's Nova. Not a product you buy. A system you build. Using components that exist right now, off the shelf, connected by one brain that runs on everything you already own.

You "Nova."
Nova "Hey."
You "What do I have today?"
Nova "Two calls this morning, a site visit at 2, and you blocked tonight. Three emails came in overnight that actually matter. The rest is noise."
You "Did Sarah respond yet?"
Nova "Not yet. It's been 3 days. Want me to draft a follow-up in your voice?"

What Nova Does

An assistant that actually assists.

Not reminders and timers. Real intelligence about your real life, delivered before you ask.

🎙
Wake Word Activation
Say "Nova" from anywhere in your home or office. She's always listening for her name, never recording anything else.
📱
Mobile Access
Trigger Nova from your phone, AirPods, or Apple Watch. She goes where you go.
🧠
Total Context
Connected to your email, calendar, Slack, financials, and knowledge graph. She knows what Siri never could.
💬
Your Voice, Not AI Voice
Drafts messages, emails, and responses that sound like you. Because she's studied how you communicate.
🎯
Drift Detection
Tracks your stated priorities against how you actually spend time. Flags when you're off track before you notice.
💡
Decision Coaching
Remembers every decision you've made, every pattern you've repeated. Brings receipts when you need them.
❤️
Energy Aware
Reads your biometrics from Apple Watch. Adjusts recommendations based on sleep, stress, and schedule density.
🏠
Physical World
Cameras and sensors at your properties feed into Nova. She detects issues and responds before they become emergencies.
