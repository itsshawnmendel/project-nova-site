PROJECT NOVA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

Overview

Project Nova ("Nova") is a personal AI assistant built and operated by Shawn Mendel. Nova is designed for private, individual use by its creator. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is handled.

What We Collect

When you interact with Nova via phone call or SMS, the following data may be processed:

  • Phone number used to contact Nova
  • Voice recordings and transcriptions of phone calls (processed by ElevenLabs)
  • Text message content sent to and from Nova's phone number
  • Timestamps of interactions

How We Use Your Data

All data collected is used solely to provide the Nova assistant service. Specifically:

  • To process and respond to voice calls and text messages
  • To improve Nova's responses and capabilities
  • To maintain conversation context for better assistance

Third-Party Services

Nova relies on the following third-party services to function:

  • Twilio — telephony and SMS delivery
  • ElevenLabs — voice processing and conversational AI

These services have their own privacy policies governing how they process data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with the third-party service providers listed above as necessary to operate Nova.

Data Retention

Interaction data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Who Uses Nova

Nova is currently a personal assistant used exclusively by its creator, Shawn Mendel. It is not a commercial product and does not serve external customers.
